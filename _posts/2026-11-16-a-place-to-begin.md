There is something nice about beginning without a grand plan.

A page does not have to explain everything at once. It can be a place to put one good question, a half-formed idea, or a detail from an ordinary day that might otherwise disappear. Over time, those small things can show a path we did not know we were on.

So this is a beginning: a small space for the work, the words, and whatever comes next.
